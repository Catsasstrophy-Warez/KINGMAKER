# DEAD HIGHWAY Research Library — Rev4

This library turns the supplied inspiration matrix into design inputs, not copied content. Every external property remains reference-only. DEAD HIGHWAY keeps original names, art, world, characters, vehicles, UI, dialogue, lore, and assets.

## Research pillars and transferable systems

### Vehicle-as-character and mechanical vulnerability
- Pacific Drive: componentized vehicle identity, garage loop, diagnostics/quirks, environmental hazards, diegetic information.
- My Summer Car: assembly and maintenance granularity; individual wear/failure; the car earns emotional value because neglect has physical consequences.
- Jalopy: vulnerable road-trip vehicle, cargo, roadside maintenance, constrained resources.
- Interstate '76: persistent post-combat vehicle state and salvage decisions.
- Project Zomboid: maintenance, towing, scavenging and practical vehicle utility.
- Car Wars / Gaslands / Darkwind: mass, placement, grip, damage, momentum and combat consequences.

DEAD HIGHWAY application: XR-13 uses persistent assemblies, node-level electrical diagnosis, fluid networks, individual condition, installation compatibility, cargo mass, tire forces, collision zones and salvage provenance. Avoid busywork by making deep interaction contextual: quick service for known/healthy systems, full procedures for diagnosis, restoration and high-risk work.

### Road economy and logistics
- Hard Truck: Apocalypse: roads and supply delivery as the connective tissue between settlements.
- Kenshi: autonomous faction/world changes and production chains.
- Space Rangers 2: simulation continues without the player and allows NPC activity to alter strategic conditions.
- Sunless Sea/Skies: fuel/range pressure makes route choice meaningful.
- Fallout 1/2: world-map travel encounters and vehicle as travel/inventory accelerator.

DEAD HIGHWAY application: settlements expose inventories and production recipes; convoys physically move resources; route danger, weather, road quality and faction control change effective supply. Prices should derive from inventory target, recent consumption, delivery confidence and isolation rather than arbitrary level scaling.

### Isometric exploration and tactical readability
- GTA 1/2: instant top-down traffic/road readability and rapid vehicle/on-foot transitions.
- Fallout 1/2, Underrail, Wasteland: authored isometric spaces, inspectable environmental storytelling and tactical information density.
- Project Zomboid: scalable isometric interiors and scavenging.
- Death Trash: modern real-time isometric movement and tactile interaction.

DEAD HIGHWAY application: modern 3D world with a readability-first camera. Roof/facade visibility, selection outlines, occlusion and camera zoom are gameplay systems, not cosmetic afterthoughts.

### Wasteland identity without imitation
- Mad Max films/game: silhouettes, resource conflict, improvised engineering, convoy spectacle and environmental scale.
- The Rover: restrained, believable vehicle decay and empty-road composition.
- Book of Eli: high-contrast wasteland lighting reference.
- Mortal Engines: enormous industrial silhouettes and machinery-as-settlement.
- Girls' Last Tour / The Road: quiet scarcity and the emotional power of ordinary intact objects.
- Redline / Twisted Metal: extreme motion and individualized combat vehicles.

DEAD HIGHWAY application: combine spectacular highway motion with long quiet intervals. Blackridge is Appalachian/industrial rather than a generic endless desert: mines, rail, river crossings, substations, mills, wooded ridges, truck stops, quarries and abandoned industrial towns.

### Survival and player-authored stories
- Kenshi / Space Rangers 2: autonomous events generate stories without requiring a quest script.
- Twilight: 2000: maintenance, fuel substitution, spare parts and towing as survival logistics.
- FAR: Lone Sails: maintaining a machine while traveling makes the vehicle itself a playable space.
- Damnation Alley: long-haul objective structure through hostile regions.

DEAD HIGHWAY application: event history records causal changes. Quests can observe the simulation, but must not secretly replace it. A broken convoy, flooded bridge or failed substation should alter subsequent world state.
