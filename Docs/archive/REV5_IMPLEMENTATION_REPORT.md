# DEAD HIGHWAY Rev5 — The World Comes Alive

Rev5 expands Rev4 outward from Kingmaker into the game-wide architecture.

## Added
- DHCharacter: attributes, skills, injuries, survival needs, equipment/inventory.
- DHFleet: generic persistent vehicles spanning motorcycles through semis, tankers, military machines and wrecks; hotwiring and towing foundations.
- DHCombat: real-time/tactical-slowdown clock, weapon state, hit and ramming calculations.
- DHNPC: persistent residents, occupations, needs, relationships, schedules/sites and dialogue flags.
- DHSettlement: resource inventories, production recipes and a 150-resident Paradise target settlement.
- DHEconomy: physical trade routes, convoy disruption hooks, scarcity-driven prices.
- DHRadio: repairable coverage towers, signal strength and broadcasts derived from world events.
- DHWorld: canonical 12-region continent atlas with differentiated climate/industry/hazard/road behavior.
- DHGameplay: executable World Comes Alive milestone chain from garage spawn through Paradise salvage trade.
- DHPresentation: isometric camera modes, simulation/render LOD policy and conditional RealityKit scene bridge.

## Scope truth
This is a compiled systems foundation, not finished production content. A continent-scale authored map, production meshes/materials/animation/audio, complete dialogue, thousands of environmental stories and shipping-quality combat still require content production and device-side Xcode work.
